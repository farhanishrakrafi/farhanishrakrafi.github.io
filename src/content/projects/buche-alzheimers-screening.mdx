## Question

Butyrylcholinesterase breaks down the neurotransmitter acetylcholine, and its activity in the brain rises as Alzheimer's disease progresses. This study asked whether compounds that occur naturally in the diet could bind and inhibit the enzyme, as starting points for new treatments.

## Approach

A library of naturally derived dietary compounds was screened in silico against the structure of butyrylcholinesterase. The full workflow, the compound set and the scoring criteria are described in the published article.
