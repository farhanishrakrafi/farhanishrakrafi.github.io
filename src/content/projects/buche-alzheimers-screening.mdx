## Overview

This study investigated naturally derived dietary compounds as potential butyrylcholinesterase inhibitors using computational methods. It was published in *Scientific Reports* in 2025.

## My contribution

I am the fourth author of the paper. The published author-contribution statement credits me with data analysis and manuscript writing.

## Scope of the evidence

The study reports computational screening. Its predictions are research findings, not evidence of clinical efficacy.
