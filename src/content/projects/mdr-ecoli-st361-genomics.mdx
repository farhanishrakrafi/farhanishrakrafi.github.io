## Question

Carbapenems are among the last dependable antibiotics for serious Gram-negative infections. This project asks how a multidrug-resistant Escherichia coli isolate from a wound infection became resistant, and which genes and mobile genetic elements explain its resistance profile.

The isolate belongs to sequence type ST361 and carries two carbapenemase genes: blaNDM-5, which encodes the NDM-5 metallo-β-lactamase, and blaOXA-181, which encodes the OXA-181 carbapenemase.

## Approach

- **Phenotype.** Antimicrobial susceptibility testing of the isolate in the laboratory.
- **Genotype.** Analysis of the whole-genome sequence to profile resistance genes, plasmids and sequence type.
- **Linking the two.** Reading the susceptibility results against the genome to see which genes account for the observed resistance.
